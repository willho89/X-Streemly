First pre-release of X-Streemly, a libretro core that plays network video streams like TV channels: channel
up/down, last channel, a channel banner, picture and sound at the stream's full resolution.

**Pre-release:** developed and tested on Linux x86_64 in RetroArch. The other builds pass the automated tests in CI
(Windows under Wine, macOS on GitHub's Macs) or are build-only (Linux ARM, Android) and haven't been tried on real
devices yet. Reports welcome.

## What it does
- **TV out of the box:** Start Core with no content shows the channels of an **HDHomeRun** tuner on your network
  (found automatically, ATSC channels numbered 3.1, 3.2 …), else [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv)'s free
  public channels for your country (core options: country, category, language), cached for offline use. Or load your
  own `.m3u` channel list, or a `.strm` file holding one stream URL. X-Streemly hosts no streams.
- **Streams:** HLS and MPEG-TS over HTTP(S); H.264, HEVC, MPEG-2 video; AAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, MP2 audio (folded to
  stereo); interlaced broadcasts deinterlaced at field rate.
- **Smooth playback:** frames shown by timestamp at your display's rate, a buffer that adapts to the stream, live-edge
  catch-up, and it rides out stalls, reconnects, mid-stream format changes and timestamp wraps; drops to a lower HLS
  quality if the connection can't keep up.
- **Audio tracks:** pick a language in the options, L2 switches instantly; optional Volume Levelling.
- **TV controls:** Up/Down channel, B last channel, L/R groups, A info, Select channel list, X/Y favourites,
  keyboard digits for channel numbers. The channel banner and cards are drawn into the picture.

## Known limits
- Live HLS only (no DASH, RTMP, RTSP, UDP multicast); no DRM'd streams; no programme guide.
- Software decoding: 1080p is fine on a desktop or Steam Deck; small ARM boards may need *Max Resolution* 720p.
- Many iptv-org streams are geo-blocked or offline at any moment; channels that don't answer are skipped for a day.
